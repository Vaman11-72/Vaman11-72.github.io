Sunderkand: Journey of Hanuman — Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

In short
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • We don't collect your name, email, location, photos, contacts or any other personal information.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Sunderkand: Journey of Hanuman (“the game”) is made by Brisbane Games, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game on Android or iPad.

What the game keeps on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your story progress, chosen language, sound settings, unlocked Extras and achievements. These are stored only on your device. We never receive them, and they're deleted when you uninstall the game.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts and no analytics or advertising services, and it doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

The app store and game engine

The game is delivered by Google Play (or the Apple App Store) and built with the Unity engine. To install the game, deliver its files and keep it running, these platforms may process limited technical information, such as your device model, operating-system version and crash reports if you've allowed your device to share them. This information isn't linked to you by us, isn't used for advertising, and is handled under the Google, Apple and Unity privacy policies.

Children

The game is made for players of all ages, including children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, and the game contains no ads, chat, links to social media or ways to contact other players. It is designed to meet Google Play's Families policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the game does with information — for example by adding optional advertising — we'll update this page, and the store's data-safety details, before that update is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
