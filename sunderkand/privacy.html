Sunderkand: Journey of Hanuman — Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short
  • No accounts and no sign-in, on any device. Brisbane Games runs no servers and collects no personal information.
  • Android: the full game, free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Nothing is collected.
  • iPhone and iPad: Adhyaya 1 is free. A one-time purchase through Apple unlocks the rest. Players who haven't bought it can choose to watch a short video, served by Google AdMob, for an instant hint or to skip a puzzle. These ads are child-safe and non-personalised. Buyers see no ads.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.

Who we are

Sunderkand: Journey of Hanuman (“the game”) is made by Brisbane Games, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game on Android, iPhone or iPad.

What the game keeps on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your story progress, chosen language, sound settings, unlocked Extras and achievements, and, on iPhone and iPad, whether you've bought the full game. These are stored only on your device. We never receive them, and they're deleted when you uninstall the game.

What we collect

Nothing. Brisbane Games has no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and the game doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. On iPhone and iPad the game never asks for permission to track you.

Purchases on iPhone and iPad

The full game is a one-time in-app purchase made through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple only tells the game whether the full game has been bought, so it can unlock it on your devices and restore it after a reinstall. Before a purchase or restore, the game asks a simple grown-up question so young children can't buy by accident. On Android there are no purchases.

Optional videos on iPhone and iPad (Google AdMob)

If you haven't bought the full game, you can choose to watch a short video for an instant hint or to skip a puzzle. Videos only play when you tap to watch one, and never once the full game is bought. They are provided by Google AdMob. Every ad request asks for:

  • non-personalised ads: ads aren't chosen from a profile of you;
  • child-directed treatment, under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws;
  • in the European Economic Area and the UK, treatment as a user under the age of consent, so no consent is collected and Google shows only limited ads;
  • ads rated G, suitable for general audiences.

To show a video, prevent fraud and count views, Google receives limited technical information from your device, such as its IP address (which gives an approximate location), device model, operating-system version and how the ad was shown. Because the game never asks for tracking permission, Google doesn't receive your device's advertising identifier. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Brisbane Games doesn't receive this information. The Android version contains no ads and no ad software.

The app store and game engine

The game is delivered by Google Play or the Apple App Store and built with the Unity engine. To install the game, deliver its files and keep it running, these platforms may process limited technical information, such as your device model, operating-system version and crash reports if you've allowed your device to share them. This information isn't linked to you by us, and it's handled under the Google, Apple and Unity privacy policies.

Children

The game is made for players of all ages, including children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The game has no chat, no social-media links and no way to contact other players. On Android it has no ads and is designed to meet Google Play's Families policy. On iPhone and iPad, the optional videos are child-directed, non-personalised and rated G, and purchases sit behind a grown-up question.

Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the game does with information, we'll update this page, and the store's privacy details, before that update is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
